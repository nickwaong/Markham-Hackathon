# Markham Link — 75-second demo script

## 0–12s | Problem

“A newcomer may arrive in Markham with a real need—health care, work, benefits, or a community connection—but not know which department owns the answer. Language and unfamiliar systems make the first month overwhelming.”

## 12–25s | Solution

“Markham Link turns one natural-language question into a calm, practical settlement plan. It is designed for newcomers, seniors and caregivers.”

## 25–47s | Live demo

Type: `我刚到加拿大，不知道怎样看医生、找工作和申请福利。`

Click **Create my plan**.

“The prototype detects the Chinese writing system locally and creates a starter plan. The first 7 days focus on the essentials: IRCC address and PR card, SIN, OHIP, banking and a Welcome Centre needs assessment.”

## 47–62s | Follow-through

Click **Within 30 days**, then tick one task.

“The next month covers CRA benefits, health navigation, language learning, work and local connection. Every card has an official next step, and completed items save on this device.”

## 62–75s | Close

“This is not another directory of websites. It is a personal, multilingual action plan that helps a newcomer move from ‘I do not know where to start’ to one clear next step.”

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p, 30fps, browser zoom 100%.
- Show the Chinese question, detected-language result, First 7 days, checkbox progress, then Within 30 days.
- Export MP4, play it through once, then upload the MP4 and the ZIP backup.
